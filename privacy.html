Privacy Policy

Effective October 21, 2026

In short: Clip Grabber runs on your own computer. We don't run servers that collect your information, there is no Clip Grabber account, and there are no analytics, ads or tracking. Information leaves your computer only when the app sends it to a service you chose to use, such as posting a clip you approved to TikTok.

This policy covers the Clip Grabber app for Windows, made up of the Clip Grabber, Clip Reviewer and Clip Poster programs, and this website. "We" means the developer of Clip Grabber. "You" means the person using the app.

2. TikTok

When you connect TikTok, you sign in on TikTok's own page. Clip Grabber never sees your TikTok password. With your permission, the app receives:

  • Basic account information (the user.info.basic permission): your TikTok ID and display name, to show which account is connected.
  • Permission to post (the video.publish permission): to post the videos you approve to your TikTok account.

Before each post, the app asks TikTok for your creator information: your nickname, username and profile picture, the privacy and interaction options your account allows, and the longest video you can post. The app uses this only to show which account you are posting to and to offer the settings TikTok allows. It is kept in memory while the app runs and is not saved, apart from the link to each post.

When you approve a clip for TikTok, the app sends TikTok the video, its caption and the settings you chose (who can see it, comments, duets, stitches and content disclosure) at the post time you chose. It then checks the post's status with TikTok.

We don't sell, share or use your TikTok information for advertising or profiling, and it never leaves your computer except to go to TikTok. Disconnecting TikTok in Clip Reviewer deletes the stored sign-in. TikTok's Privacy Policy covers what you post there.

3. YouTube

Clip Grabber uses YouTube API Services. When you connect YouTube, you sign in with Google and allow the app to upload videos (youtube.upload) and to see your channel's name (youtube.readonly), to show which channel is connected. When you approve a clip for YouTube, the app uploads the video with its title and description, set to publish at the time you chose.

By using the YouTube features of Clip Grabber, you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your information is covered by the Google Privacy Policy. You can remove Clip Grabber's access to your Google account at any time on the Google security settings page.

4. Instagram

When you connect Instagram, you paste an access token created in Meta's developer tools, with permission to read basic account information (instagram_business_basic) and to publish content (instagram_business_content_publish). The app renews the token before it expires. When you approve a clip for Instagram, the app sends the caption and asks Instagram to publish the video as a Reel.

Instagram only accepts videos from a web link. So while a clip is posting, the app shares that one video file through a temporary, randomly named Cloudflare link and closes the link as soon as Instagram has downloaded it. Nothing else on your computer can be reached through it. Meta's Privacy Policy covers what you post on Instagram.

5. Other services the app uses

  • Twitch and YouTube, for downloading: when you add a VOD link, the app downloads the video, its public chat and its stream details. See Twitch's Privacy Notice and the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Cloudflare: carries the temporary link used for Instagram posts. See Cloudflare's Privacy Policy.
  • GitHub: hosts this website and the installer download. GitHub may record visitors' IP addresses in its server logs. See the GitHub Privacy Statement. We add no analytics or cookies to this website.
  • Anthropic (optional): only if you add your own Anthropic API key, the app sends a clip's transcript, the stream title and the text of chat messages around the moment (without usernames) to Anthropic's Claude to suggest a title and description. See Anthropic's Privacy Policy. Without a key, nothing is sent and the app drafts titles itself.

Each of these services handles information under its own policy.

6. Keeping and deleting your information

Everything above stays on your computer until you delete it. We hold no copy, so there is nothing for us to delete on our side. To delete your information and remove the app's access:

  • Disconnect accounts: in Clip Reviewer, disconnect TikTok, YouTube or Instagram. This deletes the stored sign-in from your computer.
  • Remove access on each platform: remove Clip Grabber under the apps and permissions settings of your TikTok, Google or Instagram account.
  • Delete everything: uninstall Clip Grabber in Windows Settings, then delete the folders %APPDATA%\Clip Grabber (settings and sign-ins) and Videos\Clip Grabber (videos, clips and analysis).

Videos already posted stay on TikTok, YouTube or Instagram until you delete them there.

7. Security

Connections to TikTok, Google, Meta, Cloudflare and Anthropic use HTTPS. Sign-ins are stored as files in your Windows user profile, so anyone who can use your Windows account could use them. Keep your computer password-protected, and disconnect an account in Clip Reviewer if you think a sign-in has been exposed.

8. Children

Clip Grabber is not meant for children under 13. TikTok, YouTube and Instagram also set their own minimum ages for posting.
