Turn your stream VODs into ready-to-post vertical clips

Clip Grabber is a Windows app that finds the moments your chat went wild, cuts them into captioned 9:16 clips, and posts the ones you approve to TikTok, YouTube Shorts and Instagram Reels.

How it works

From a finished stream to scheduled posts in four steps. Nothing goes out until you approve it.

  1. Add a VOD

    Paste a Twitch or YouTube link, or drop in a video file. Clip Grabber downloads the video and its chat.

  2. Find the moments

    It scores every 10 seconds of the stream by chat activity and audio, and lists the top moments with what chat said.

  3. Review the clips

    Watch each vertical clip, trim it, edit the title and caption, then approve or reject it.

  4. Post on schedule

    Pick a time and the platforms. Clip Grabber posts at that time and shows a link to each post.

Features

Built for streamers and the clippers who work with them.

Finds the hype

Laughter, hype words and "clip it" in chat, plus spikes in loudness, push the best moments to the top. Gift-sub spam is ignored.

Vertical, ready to post

Each clip is 1080×1920 with the facecam on top and gameplay below. Drag a box to set where the camera is.

Captions that pop

Speech is transcribed on your PC and shown word by word, with a different outline color for each voice.

Review before posting

See the chat behind each moment, the transcript and a suggested title. Trim, edit, approve or reject.

Three platforms, one queue

Schedule clips to TikTok, YouTube Shorts and Instagram Reels. Failed posts show the reason and can be retried.

Sorted for you

Clips are filed by game and streamer, using the stream's category at that moment.

You stay in control

Clip Grabber runs on your own computer and only posts what you approve.

  • Nothing is posted until you approve the clip and its post time.
  • On TikTok you choose who can see each video and whether comments, duets and stitches are allowed. Nothing is pre-selected.
  • You can mark promotional videos with TikTok's content disclosure.
  • No watermark or logo is added to your videos.
  • Your sign-ins stay on your computer. Disconnect any account at any time.
  • No Clip Grabber account, no analytics, no ads. See the Privacy Policy.

Getting started

  1. Run Clip Grabber Setup.exe. Clip Grabber and Clip Reviewer appear on your desktop.
  2. Open Clip Reviewer and connect the TikTok, YouTube or Instagram accounts you want to post to.
  3. Open Clip Grabber, paste a link to one of your stream VODs and press Start.

Only clip and post streams you own or have permission to use. See the Terms of Service. Questions or problems: livestream.clips97@hotmail.com.

Requirements

  • Windows 11, 64-bit
  • An NVIDIA graphics card is recommended. Without one, captions and encoding run on the processor, more slowly.
  • About 5 GB of free disk space, plus room for your VODs
  • A TikTok, YouTube or Instagram account to post to
